Privacy Policy

Information and permissions

ECHO PUBLISHER accesses only the information and permissions authorized by users when they connect their social media accounts.

How information is used

Authentication tokens and authorized account information are used solely to provide account connection, management, and publishing functionality requested by the user.

TikTok integration

When a user connects TikTok, ECHO PUBLISHER uses the permissions authorized by that user to identify the connected account and support approved video publishing or draft upload workflows.

Sale of personal information

ECHO PUBLISHER does not sell personal information.

User control

Users may revoke access to their connected social media accounts at any time through the relevant platform controls.

Data deletion

Users may request deletion by following the Data Deletion instructions.
